🔒 Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

📅 Last Updated: September 2026

At SyedGames, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website.

🔑 Our Commitment: We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email address (only if you voluntarily provide it via contact forms).
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on the site, and game interactions.
  • Device Data: Browser type, IP address, operating system, and device type.
  • Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our gaming services.
  • To improve user experience and game recommendations.
  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • To send occasional updates (only if you opt-in).
  • To analyze site traffic and usage patterns.

3. Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our content.

You can control cookie preferences in your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services to improve our site, including:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying relevant advertisements. Google has its own privacy policy.
  • Game Platforms: Games are embedded from platforms like CrazyGames and GameFlare. They may collect usage data.
  • Analytics: We may use tools like Google Analytics to understand site traffic.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

  • You can request access to the data we hold about you.
  • You can request correction or deletion of your data.
  • You can opt-out of marketing communications at any time.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.

By using our website, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
